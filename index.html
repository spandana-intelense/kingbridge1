Our Heritage

Three Decades of
Transformation

1989

Designed by legendary architect Arthur Erickson in collaboration with Murray Koffler, the campus was conceived as one of Canada's first world-class health and wellness destinations — King Ranch.

1992

CIBC transformed the property into a private, state-of-the-art leadership development and training facility, establishing its heritage in executive learning.

2001

Purchased by John Abele, retired co-founder of Boston Scientific, who shaped the vision of Kingbridge as a global residential convening destination for collaborative innovation.

2021

Entrusted to the Pathak Family Trust, evolving the vision into a living learning space designed to demonstrate and accelerate innovations with positive global impact.
